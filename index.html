About Our Clinic

Why Smile Garden is Kathmandu's Trusted Choice

Located in Old Baneshwor, Smile Garden Dental Clinic has been serving Kathmandu with exceptional dental care since 2009. Our facility combines cutting-edge technology with a warm environment.

We believe everyone deserves a healthy, confident smile. Our team of experienced dentists are committed to personalized, pain-free care.
