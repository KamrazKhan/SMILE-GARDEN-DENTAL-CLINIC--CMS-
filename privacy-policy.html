Smile Garden Dental Clinic ("we," "us," or "our"), located at Old Baneshwor, Kathmandu, Nepal, is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard data when you use our website at smilegardendental.com or book appointments through our online system.

🔒 By using our website and booking appointments, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.
1

Information We Collect

When you book an appointment or contact us, we collect the following information:

  • Full Name — to identify you as a patient
  • Phone Number — to confirm appointments and send reminders
  • Email Address — for appointment confirmation (optional)
  • Age — to provide appropriate dental care
  • Preferred Service & Doctor — to assign the right specialist
  • Preferred Date & Time — to schedule your appointment
  • Symptoms / Notes — to prepare our dentists before your visit

We also automatically collect basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited — for website analytics purposes only.

2

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To confirm and manage your dental appointment
  • To contact you via phone/WhatsApp for appointment reminders
  • To assign your appointment to the appropriate doctor
  • To improve our clinic services and patient experience
  • To send festival offers or health tips (only if you opt-in)
  • To comply with Nepal's health regulations if required by law
We will NEVER use your information for unsolicited marketing or sell it to third parties.
3

Data Storage & Security

Your appointment data is securely stored using Supabase, a cloud database provider with enterprise-grade security. Specific measures include:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS 256-bit encryption
  • Database access is restricted to authorized clinic staff only
  • Row-level security policies prevent unauthorized data access
  • Data is stored on secure servers (EU region, Supabase infrastructure)
  • Admin panel is password-protected and accessible only to clinic staff

We retain your appointment data for 2 years for medical record purposes, after which it is deleted from our systems.

4

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data in these limited circumstances:

  • Within Clinic: Only dentists and authorized staff who need it to provide your care
  • Legal Requirement: If required by Nepal government or court order
  • Emergency: If required to protect your health and safety
  • Service Providers: Our hosting/database providers (Netlify, Supabase) who are bound by confidentiality agreements
5

Your Rights

As a patient, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to medical record laws)
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from any marketing communications at any time
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with us or relevant authorities in Nepal

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@smilegardendental.com or call +977 980-1234567.

6

Cookies & Technology

Our website uses minimal cookies and localStorage for:

  • Remembering your appointment ID (stored locally on your device only)
  • Basic website analytics to improve user experience
  • No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are used

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some website features may not function properly.

7

Children's Privacy

We provide pediatric dental services and may collect information about minor patients. For patients under 16 years of age:

  • Appointments must be booked by a parent or legal guardian
  • A parent/guardian must be present during the dental visit
  • We apply extra care and security to data involving minors
8

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
